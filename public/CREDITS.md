# Asset credits

## Background music

**"Seaside Village"** from the *Ocean Music Pack* by **Leonardo Paz (Leohpaz)**
Source: https://leohpaz.itch.io/ocean-music-pack

Served as `public/music-seaside.ogg` and `public/music-seaside.mp3`, converted
from the pack's original `02_Seaside_Village.wav`. The itch.io listing names
that track "Village by the Ocean"; the filename inside the pack is the name
used here.

### Licence, checked 5 September 2026

The downloaded pack folder carries no licence file, so the terms were taken
from the pack's own itch.io page, which is the rights holder's published
statement about it. The page describes the pack as royalty free and states
that the tracks are free to use. Asked directly on that page whether the music
could be used in a commercial project, the author answered: "Yes, it's free to
use. Please, credit me (Leonardo Paz)" (Leohpaz, comment on the pack page).

**What this means for us:** commercial use is permitted, and the author asks to
be credited. A credit line in a repository file is not something a player ever
sees, so the credit is shown inside the game itself, on the privacy and credits
page linked from the footer of every screen.

**Still worth doing before any paid release:** save a dated copy or screenshot
of that page. A statement in a comment thread is a reasonable basis, but it is
not a licence file, and pages can change.
